Farhad V. Farahani

Ph.D. Candidate in Economics, University of South Florida

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the University of South Florida. My research is in health economics and public policy. I study how Medicaid and Medicare policies affect access to care, health outcomes, and disparities, with current work on maternal and infant health and long-term care. My work uses quasi-experimental methods, including staggered difference-in-differences and instrumental variables.

Job Market Paper

Helping Hands, Healthier Infants: The Effect of Medicaid Doula Coverage Mandates on Birth Outcomes

SEA Annual Meeting, Houston, Nov 2026

Abstract

I evaluate the staggered 2021–2024 rollout of Medicaid doula coverage using 32.1 million births. I find no average effect on low birth weight, but a decline of about 0.5 percentage points (5%) among Black mothers in the longest-exposed states. Coverage roughly doubles the doula workforce.

Returns to U.S. and Foreign Experience among Immigrant Men: Evidence from IPUMS Microdata

2025 · arXiv:2512.18827
